Relational Schema Representation

The conceptual model is formalized into Third Normal Form (3NF) across three primary relation schemas:

  • ORGANIZERS (PK organizer_id, name, department, contact_email, created_at)
  • VENUES (PK venue_id, UQ name, building, CHK capacity, total_bookings, created_at)
  • BOOKINGS (PK booking_id, FK venue_id, FK organizer_id, event_name, booking_date, CHK status, created_at, UQ [venue_id, booking_date])

Entity & Cardinality Analysis

  • Organizers Entity: Represents campus departments, faculty heads, and student bodies holding legitimate event booking privileges.
  • Venues Entity: Represents physical university venues. Enforces unique naming and positive seating capacities (capacity > 0). Maintains a trigger-updated counter total_bookings.
  • Bookings Entity (Relational Junction): Links an organizer to a venue on a designated calendar date. Enforces referential integrity with cascading deletes and a strict composite uniqueness constraint.
  • Cardinality Ratios:
    • Organizers to Bookings is 1 : N (One organizer can schedule multiple events across different dates).
    • Venues to Bookings is 1 : N (One venue hosts multiple events over time, but strictly 1 booking per date).
